You Mean More Than Words Can Say 🤍 🌻

Happy birthday, my dearest friend. Happy birthday to someone I’m endlessly grateful to have in my life. You came into my life as a friend, but somehow, you became family. You are the kind of person I never want to take for granted the one whose presence makes life feel a little warmer, a little lighter, and a lot more meaningful. Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every little moment, and for simply being you. I may not say it enough, but I love and appreciate you more than words could ever hold. I’m truly grateful that life allowed our paths to cross.

My little wish for you

I hope this new chapter of your life brings you the kind of happiness your heart has always deserved. I wish you gentle days, genuine smiles, beautiful surprises, and dreams that slowly turn into reality. May Allah protect your heart, guide your steps, and answer the prayers you whisper when no one else is listening. I hope you never forget how precious you are, how loved you are, and how much your existence means to the people who truly care about you. And wherever life takes you, I hope it takes you somewhere beautiful. You deserve all the good things this life has to offer.🤍

“Keep blooming, just like a sunflower.”

Page 03

TILL JANNAH 🤍

I don’t know where life will take us, or how much we may change as the years pass. But one thing I hope never changes is the place you have in my heart. Thank you for being more than a friend. Thank you for being the sister I was never born with, but somehow found in this life. And if Allah allows me one beautiful wish beyond this world, it would be this: May we remain sisters in this dunya, and may Allah reunite us in Jannah. No distance. No endings. No goodbyes. Just us, looking back at this life and realizing how beautifully Allah wrote our story. Happy birthday, my precious soul. I’m forever grateful for you. Till Jannah, insyaAllah. 🤍
